Our Crown Care Services

Every hair service at The Afro Crown Beauty is executed with delicate technique and premium natural products. We take the time to analyze your hair density, porosity and scalp health prior to styling to ensure your natural crown thrives under our care.

Our Protective Braid & Twist Artistry offers flawless, low tension protective styles that safeguard your edges and promote length retention. All braiding services include a complimentary wash and moisturizing prep treatment.

Our Deep Hydration & Scalp Restoration features targeted spa-style treatments that revive dry, damaged coils and clear scalp irritation.

Our Loc Grooming & Maintenance provides specialized care designed to keep starter locs, mature locs, and microlocs clean, neat, and strong.
